Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Short summary (Hinglish): Bina login ke tumhara data sirf tumhare phone me rehta hai. Google login karoge to data tumhare account se jude cloud (Google Firebase) me save hota hai taaki dusre phone par mile. Hum tumhara data bechte nahi hain.

1. What HisabSaathi is

HisabSaathi is a free bookkeeping web app for personal and small-shop records (income, expenses and udhaar/khata). It is not a bank, payment provider or financial advisor.

2. Data we handle

Without login: your entries, customer names and phone numbers, shop name, logo and UPI ID are stored only in your browser on your device. We do not receive them.

With Google login: we receive your name and email from Google Sign-In, and we store a copy of your app data (entries, udhaar records, shop details, referral code and referral records) in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so it can sync across your devices.

3. How we use data

Only to run the app: sign you in, sync your data, apply referral rewards and manage Premium membership. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads at present.

4. Third parties

Google Firebase (login and database) processes data on our behalf under Google's terms. WhatsApp and UPI buttons open the WhatsApp or UPI app on your phone; we do not read or send those messages. The UPI QR code is generated on your device.

5. Your customers' data

If you store your customers' names and phone numbers, you are responsible for using them lawfully and only to run your shop.

6. PIN lock

The PIN is stored only on your device (as a hash) and is for basic privacy, not strong security. If you forget it, the app data on that device may need to be reset, so take regular backups.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you clear your browser data. Cloud data stays until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion of your cloud data, contact us as below with your login email.

8. Children

The app is not meant for children under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.

10. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: github.com/DKSARKAR1/hisabsathi/issues
